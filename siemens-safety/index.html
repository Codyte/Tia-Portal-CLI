Siemens Safety groups, signatures and printouts by command

Safety configuration is auditable by design, and these verbs read exactly what an audit asks for: the groups, the settings, the collective signature, the printout, and the generated base ID.

12 verbs of 348 · TIA Portal V19, V20 and V21

tia safety-info [--plc N]
read-only: the F-program of the PLC
tia list-safety-groups [--plc N] [--name X]
read-only: the runtime groups of the F-program
tia create-safety-group --name N [--block B] [--apply]
a runtime group brings its own F-OB
tia delete-safety-group --name N [--apply]
takes the F-OB of the group with it
tia set-safety-setting [--name A] [--value V] [--apply]
one setting by attribute name, looked up in SafetySettings, then AssignmentOfBlockNumbers, then GlobalSettings
tia safety-signatures [--plc N] [--like X]
read-only: the F-signature of each block (SafetySignatureProvider), against safety-info which gives the signatures of the whole PROGRAM
tia export-safety-printout [--plc N] [--out FILE] [--printer pdf|xps] [--kind all|compact] [--layout NAME]
the safety printout the acceptance test asks for
tia generate-safety-baseid [--plc N] [--apply]
generates the F-BaseID and distributes it to every PROFIsafe type 3 F-IO
tia list-safety-tests [--name X]
read-only: activation tests of the Safety Validation Assistant, the safety functions of each test, the conditions of each function, and the validity the Portal itself computes) [verb: V21 only
tia create-safety-test --name N [--device D] [--apply]
without --device it answers with the possible evaluation devices
tia delete-safety-test --name N [--apply]
removes the test WITH its safety functions
tia export-safety-test-report --name N [--file F.xlsx] [--out DIR]
the .xlsx report of one activation test (ActivationTestPrintout)
